# WAIRC-2026 v0.10.0

This release makes repository-authored synthetic benchmark fixtures executable
verification inputs without changing the competition data or submission
contracts.

## Highlights

- `wairc benchmark verify-fixture` replays either `benchmark-fixture-v1`
  manifest and checks its redistribution metadata, generated manifest fields,
  report schema, and deterministic SHA-256 signature.
- CI replays both the `cpu-smoke` and `robustness-small` fixtures in the
  quality job, while source-distribution checks continue to verify that the
  fixtures and release documentation are packaged.

## Compatibility

The command generates ordinary synthetic benchmark outputs only under the
selected local output directory. It does not download competition data or
trained checkpoints and does not intentionally change the 0..8 label mapping,
interleaved IQ parsing, missing-node semantics, `stft-v1`, training, inference,
checkpoint fields, or submission serialization. Fixture signatures are
reproducibility checks on generated synthetic data, not real-data benchmarks or
competition scores.

## Verification

The release gates and exact CI run, commit, and artifact hashes will be recorded
here after the release commit and tag complete their checks.

## External assets

Competition data, trained checkpoints, generated caches, and private test
labels are not included. Users must obtain restricted assets through their
original channel and follow the applicable terms. Dependencies retain their
own licenses.
