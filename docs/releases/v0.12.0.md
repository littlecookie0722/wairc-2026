# WAIRC-2026 v0.12.0

This release adds a shared seed contract for reproducible CPU-first research
workflows without changing the competition data or submission contracts.

## Highlights

- `wairc_rf.set_reproducible_seed` validates one integer seed and applies it to
  Python, NumPy, and PyTorch CPU/CUDA random generators.
- Training DataLoader workers initialize Python and NumPy from PyTorch's worker
  seed, covering NumPy-based crop and SpecAugment operations.
- Legacy training and k-fold inference seed helpers remain available and route
  through the shared implementation.

## Compatibility

Training keeps the existing cuDNN benchmark preference by default. K-fold
inference requests deterministic cuDNN selection. Fold split inputs, loader
shuffle policy, `stft-v1`, labels, checkpoint fields, inference rules, and
submission serialization remain unchanged.

The helper is a reproducibility aid, not a promise of bit-for-bit equality
across every CUDA operator, device, driver, or dependency version. The release
does not claim real-data accuracy, GPU training results, or competition scores.
