# Code of Conduct

WAIRC-2026 welcomes respectful, technically grounded participation from people
with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific when discussing code, experiments, and results.
- Distinguish verified evidence from assumptions, especially for benchmark and
  leaderboard claims.
- Respect dataset licenses, privacy boundaries, and responsible use of RF data.
- Give contributors credit and accept good-faith corrections.
- Keep issue and review discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure
of private information, fabricated results, and sharing data or credentials
without authorization are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact options on the maintainer's public GitHub profile. Security
vulnerabilities should follow `SECURITY.md` instead of this process.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that violate these guidelines
and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the
project.
